Privacy Career Paths

The privacy field is growing rapidly, with opportunities across industries. Below are common roles and their responsibilities to help you understand potential career paths.

Supports privacy operations, conducts assessments, and monitors compliance activities.
Oversees privacy initiatives, manages policies, and coordinates cross‑functional compliance efforts.
Ensures regulatory compliance, manages risk, and serves as the point of contact for regulators.
Provides legal guidance on data protection laws, contracts, and regulatory obligations.
Focuses on data governance, risk assessments, and operational controls.
Addresses privacy risks in AI systems, including LLMs, model training data, and automated decision‑making.
