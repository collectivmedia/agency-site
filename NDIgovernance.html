AI Security & Governance

As organizations adopt AI and LLM‑powered systems, governance becomes essential. NDI helps you deploy AI responsibly, securely, and in alignment with your organizational values and regulatory requirements.

🤖 AI Governance Frameworks
Define the policies, controls, and oversight structures needed to manage AI systems throughout their lifecycle.

🔐 AI Security & Threat Modeling
Identify and mitigate risks unique to AI systems — including model poisoning, prompt injection, data leakage, and unauthorized model access.

🧠 LLM Risk Management
Address the challenges of large language models, including hallucinations, bias, over‑reliance, and uncontrolled data exposure.

🛠 Responsible AI Operations (RAIO)
Implement monitoring, auditing, and continuous evaluation processes to ensure AI systems behave as intended.

🚀 AI Enablement & Adoption
NDI specializes in facilitating organizations with using AI to its fullest capabilities, but doing so securely. We help teams adopt AI tools confidently while maintaining strong governance and security boundaries.
